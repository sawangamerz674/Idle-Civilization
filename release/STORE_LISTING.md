# Idle Civilization - CrazyGames submission text

**Title:** Idle Civilization
**Short description:** Grow a tiny Stone Age village into a futuristic empire. Build, research, advance through five eras and start again with Civilization Legacy.
**Genre / tags:** idle, incremental, clicker, strategy, city builder, management, casual
**Controls:** Mouse or touch only. Click or tap buildings, research and upgrades. On phones drag the scene to look around. No keyboard needed.
**Orientation:** Landscape and portrait (a fixed mobile layout is provided for both).
**Age rating:** PEGI 3 content (no violence, no gambling, no user-generated chat). Within the PEGI 12 requirement.
**Languages:** English.
**Privacy:** The game collects no personal data. Progress is stored in the browser and, when available, with the CrazyGames data module.
**Launch type:** Full Launch (SDK: gameplayStart/Stop, rewarded ads, data module).
**Covers:** `release/store/` (1920x1080, 800x1200, 800x800). Icon: `assets/icons/icon-512.png`.
